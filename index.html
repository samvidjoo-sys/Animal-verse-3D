Why AnimalVerse 3D?
To make wildlife knowledge exciting and accessible — especially the places animals call home, the threats they face, and the species we have already lost. This is a starter collection that can grow as we add verified research and proper 3D models.

🔎

Accuracy comes first.
Conservation labels can change as assessments are updated. Locations describe known native or historical ranges, not necessarily every place an animal has ever been recorded. Extinct-animal visuals must be clearly labelled as artistic or AI reconstructions — never presented as real photographs.
